OPTICAL CHANNEL ARRAY / REPLICATION PROTOCOL
Nominal dimensions for the optical channel array study.

Prototype: 18 columns x 14 rows, 252 hollow tubes, 8.0 mm OD, 7.4 mm
nominal clear ID, 8.05 mm pitch, 25 mm depth. Measure the tubes before
laying out a final frame. The printable drawing is a center-and-placement
guide, not a requirement to drill 252 holes with only 0.05 mm webs.

Fast test: First make a 7 x 7 coupon from straight opaque tube stock.
Cut square and deburr using a jig. Stand the pieces on a flat plate against
a square fence, and cinch them into a shallow cardboard collar with tape.
For the full panel use a 144.9 x 112.7 mm nominal clear frame opening with
5 mm rails on each side and an open back. Clamp the outer tubes gently.
Seal gaps between tubes with removable opaque putty if they show a
distracting second pattern; don't plug the bores. Try differently colored
walls separately. For a full array plan on about 6.3 meters of tube,
plus cutting waste.

Shoot: camera > tube panel > illuminated subject. Start with the panel
as near the lens as focusing and coverage allow. Aim through the center
and expose for the face or chart behind it. Lock camera exposure/focus
before comparing; rock the panel slowly rather than panning the subject.
Compare 0 degrees and roughly 15 degrees; try a 15 mm segment if too
dark, or a 35 mm segment if the angular effect is too mild.

A straight opaque tube limits the overlap of its near and far apertures
by L*tan(theta). For ID 7.4 mm and L 25 mm, a single ideal tube loses its
direct view at roughly 16.5 degrees. That isn't a camera-wide cutoff:
perspective makes the local angle different across the panel, and real
walls scatter light. The renders compare camera positions using the
same geometry and grid. Look at the center band and how the visible
window moves sideways in the off-axis view.

Blender: the file contains the actual annular bore meshes (24 segments
per tube), three cameras, lights and a chartreuse test chart. It does not
need external textures. To rebuild with altered dimensions change
cols/rows/pitch/depth/OD/ID near the top of build_panel.py and run it with
a local Blender executable in background mode.
